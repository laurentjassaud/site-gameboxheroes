What makes the boxes special

Assisted mode

Every box will offer an assisted mode that checks every action is legal. Rules will be implemented including the Questions & Answers from the BoardGameGeek forum. Of course, a free mode will let players play without assistance.

Better head-to-head games

A richer experience with double-blind play, blitz-style timed games and every variant, including fan-made ones.

Solo play

For solo games, the box interface runs the whole solo engine faithfully. Several levels of control let you roll the dice yourself on the various flowcharts, or let the interface resolve the engine’s responses so you can focus on strategy.

For games designed for two players, work on a symbolic AI is under way: it will provide a capable opponent in every game.
